Core Management Endpoints

Menu & Categories

Public / Manager

Browse dishes, filter by category or slug, search by title, and sort by price. Managers can create, update, and remove items.

GET /api/menu-items/
GET /api/categories/
POST /api/menu-items/

Table Reservations

Authenticated

Reserve tables with validation against overlapping reservations for the same table and time slot. Customers manage their own bookings; managers can view all.

GET /api/bookings/
POST /api/bookings/
GET /api/bookings/{id}/

Cart & Order Pipeline

Role-Based

Customers add items to cart and convert to orders. Managers assign delivery crew members. Delivery crew view and fulfill deliveries.

GET /api/cart/menu-items/
POST /api/orders/
PATCH /api/orders/{id}/

About Little Lemon

Based in Chicago, Illinois, Little Lemon is run by two Italian brothers, Mario and Adrian, who came to the United States to pursue their shared passion of cooking. Mario focuses on the authentic Mediterranean recipes learned from their grandmother, while Adrian leads the restaurant's contemporary dining experience and digital ordering technology.


This Django REST Framework web application serves as the production-ready backend supporting online table booking, dynamic menu display, order management, and secure role-based staff operations with MySQL.
